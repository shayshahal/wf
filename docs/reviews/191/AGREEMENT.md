## Agreed
- **Behavior.** Notification and tracker completion are separate facts. Keep `(posted)` meaning “notification sent,” including legacy notes. An OPEN PR never grants delivered status. Only confirmed delivery permits merged-status reconciliation; previously posted sections still participate, without resending their comment or selected attachments.
- **Choice.** Keep a minimal durable, per-ticket completion acknowledgement bound to the confirmed delivery identity. Write it only after reading the actual desired tracker status. On retry, read current status before attempting transition; if already delivered (including a transition whose local acknowledgement was interrupted), acknowledge without requiring another transition. Reject using `(posted)` for both facts or clearing it to force another post: both lose recovery meaning or risk duplicate notification. A recorded confirmed acknowledgement completes this round's obligation; this is not continuous monitoring of later external tracker changes.
- **Unknown and partial failure.** Missing/unreadable/malformed/unknown tracker status remains incomplete, with actionable retry. A successful transition request alone is insufficient: read back the desired state. Keep successful ticket acknowledgements when another ticket fails; retain the note, proof and remaining work. No blanket repost. Existing #148 exact picture selection and attachment/comment deduplication remain intact, including interruption after comment.
- **Routing and cleanup.** `next` must route pending post-merge obligations back to the project post/reconciliation command, not offer reap. Normal cleanup must refuse incomplete or unknown post-merge work even if invoked directly. Reuse the existing explicit intentional-discard policy; do not alter reap retry/journal mechanics (#192). A legacy `(posted)` flag alone never supplies merged-status evidence; restore/check delivery identity and reconcile it, or remain incomplete.
- **Ownership and freshness.** JewelryX owns status names, transitions and tracker response parsing. Core consumes project-exported neutral pending/completion facts and persists only the minimal bound acknowledgement. Preserve existing delivery/approval/correction/changed-product fences and unknown-versus-absent semantics. Network calls run outside state locks; revalidate round, delivery, ticket/note subject and relevant guards before remote mutation and acknowledgement. Stale observations cannot settle another delivery or overwrite concurrent recovery work. Retries must converge after crashes without duplicate notification/upload.
- **Excluding.** No automatic merge, production tracker mutation as testing, second delivery engine, job engine, new tracker platform, removed phase revival, global configuration/hooks, JewelryX setup/serve/database lifecycle, #192 reap retry redesign, #193 decision projection, #159 worker launching or suite optimization. Genuine separate findings are recorded, not implemented.
- **Verification.** Keep one continuous public-command lifecycle and test failure after comment, success-before-acknowledgement crash, repeated post/delivery reconciliation, already-delivered status, unavailable/unknown/malformed status, legacy posted notes and multi-ticket partial completion. Assert no premature transition/reap/deletion, lost recovery work, duplicate comment/upload or weakened identity guards. Add corruption/stale/concurrent authority cases where the new receipt crosses a durable boundary. Run focused affected selfchecks plus tsc/lint at the final product/test head, including relevant #190 regression coverage; disclose failures and NOT MEASURED limits, never reuse old-head green for repaired behavior.
- **Not asked for.** None. Local task records substitute only for the JewelryX-only runtime's inability to represent wf development; they do not substitute for human agreement, independent assessment or final publication approval.
- **T2 walk.** No product UI/stack. Show scoped diff, controlled lifecycle logs, final-head focused evidence and a fresh independent assessment. At most two autonomous within-agreement repair rounds before escalating consequential unresolved choices; scope/contract changes return to T1 immediately.
